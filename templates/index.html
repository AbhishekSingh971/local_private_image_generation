Private Image AI

Train a reusable face identity with LoRA, generate portraits, and optionally create short AI videos.
Step 1 · Identity dataset

Prepare one face

Use your own photos or photos of a person who has consented. Choose clear images of the same person with varied angles, expressions and lighting. Avoid group photos, strong filters, sunglasses and near-duplicates.

Up to 30 photos per upload · max 8 MB each. Images are re-encoded to remove EXIF metadata.
Checking saved dataset…
Aim for 15–30 good photos. This app stores them locally; it does not upload them to an inference service.
Step 2 · Train once

Create your face LoRA

Training reads the saved photos and the local Stable Diffusion 1.5 model, then saves an identity adapter for reuse.

Run this in the project folder with its virtual environment activated. Training requires an NVIDIA GPU and CUDA-enabled PyTorch; the bundled installer installs CPU-only PyTorch.
After training, click “Refresh face models” and select your saved model below. Include the trigger token in prompts, e.g. “photo of sks person”.
Step 3 · Photo generation

Generate a photo

Choose a trained face LoRA to reuse the identity in new scenes.
No reference = text-to-image. With a reference = image-to-image edit.
Reference image preview (optional)
Only used with a reference image.
Optional · Video generation

Animate the trained identity

Uses AnimateDiff with the local SD 1.5 model and selected face LoRA. Identity and motion consistency can vary by frame.

Video needs an NVIDIA GPU, CUDA-enabled PyTorch and local motion-adapter weights. The current CPU-only laptop setup cannot run it.
